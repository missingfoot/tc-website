Many different people make up The Collective. From entrepreneurs to creatives, developers to teachers, it’s the unique stories and skill sets that make collective living full of opportunity. To meet different people, collaborate, and learn something new.

In our new video series<em>,</em> we get to know some of these people and learn about what drives them.

First up, filmmaker and member Ollie chats to Adam. Originally from Sydney, Adam moved to London a few years ago. Since then, he has worked as a personal trainer and set up his own PT business while living at The Collective Old Oak. Here, he talks fitness, health, and the ups and downs of going out on your own.
