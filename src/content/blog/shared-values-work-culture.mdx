Communities are strongest when they have a set of shared values. And in a similar way, it’s the same for our team. Last year our CEO, Reza Merchant, invited the company to his family home where we were all welcomed by his parents and family members The gathering was a celebration of our new company mission statement and values, which we had created together as a team.

This wasn’t the first gathering at the Merchant household. Every summer there’s a buzz about the annual get-together and the opportunity to celebrate our hard work, reflecting on how far The Collective has come. Joining together with our CEO’s family is a powerful reminder of where we started, and the community that companies can foster internally.

Over the last six months our values have been at the forefront of our company agenda. The Collective took a collaborative approach and through a number of creative events and get togethers our values were formed, fine tuned, (sometimes argued about) and finally agreed upon. Our values are **Community**, **Fearless**, **Grow**, **Respect**, and <strong>Balance.</strong>

Each one of these are special, because they were chosen together and they echo the importance of what The Collective stands for and believes in. They don’t just shape us as a team, but also play a big part in our recruitment process. In other words, they help to determine who joins our mission.

When I talk to candidates on the phone or face to face, I scope out their appreciation of the startup world. I do this because it’s important to understand that we are a different kind of company, meaning we may not have as many processes in place as others. I believe that if this fills people with excitement, there’s a good chance they’re going to enjoy the opportunities available to them. Because there are so many ways to be 100% **Fearless** in our approach to work, especially when it comes to building and innovating our own processes. It’s two fold, we also have the chance to grow as individuals because what we create is unique. For some, it really is the ultimate chance to learn and develop skills.

> These values are what drive us and what make us who we are at The Collective. If you believe in these values, we believe in you.

But what about the *fun* stuff?

Recruitment isn’t the only area influenced by our values. Take our working environment, which we’re proud to say boasts a few great perks that are reflective of our shared vision. Such as our Flexi Wednesdays, where team members can work from home or their local coffee shop if your role allows for it. This echoes the importance of **Balance**, something that’s also encouraged through the popular yoga classes we hold at the office every Thursday. It’s a precious opportunity to head up to the terrace (Yes… we have an outdoor terrace) and escape for an hour to collect your thoughts and process the day.

We also encourage fearlessness through our company nomadic work trips! We do this because we truly believe that we can change the way people live and work together. The Collective will cover the cost (within reason, of course) of flights and accommodation for a small group to spend a few days in a city in Europe. The idea is that we discover first hand what it’s like to work remotely from another country whilst researching the potential for co-living opportunities there.

I can’t not mention the most recent personal development policy we implemented for employees. Naturally this aligns with our value, **Grow**. We want people to have the opportunity to develop their skills either specific to their role or in an area that will have profound impact on their work. Uniquely, we don’t purely invest in role specific professional development. The initiative is driven by personal development in the interest of growing as people and adding value to our community.

Now, the initiatives don’t stop there… the above are just a few of my favourites that I believe echo the core values and make us unique.

Aside from the perks and our amazing community, we are growing quickly and there are lots of opportunities to come help shape collective living.
