Right now at The Collective Old Oak, six fellows are taking part in our first ever [Community Facilitator Programme](https://medium.com/@collectivist/a-week-in-the-life-of-our-first-community-fellows-1f24119429e3). That means they’re on the ground for the next couple of months, supporting our members in making their home amazing. Along the way, they’re on a peer-led journey exploring tools to foster a brilliant community experience.

We’re spotlighting each fellow in a series of profiles. Today, meet Laura!

<Figure src="/images/blog/meet-the-fellows-laura/01-the-collective-old-oak-west-london-co-living-commu.webp" alt="the collective old oak west london co-living community" caption="“It is important to operate from the heart.”" />

Laura is a creative entrepreneur, animal lover, spiritualist and connector. Her journey has taken her from the UN headquarters in New York to the depths of the Amazon rainforest, in a bid to address the discord and suffering on our planet. Early on in her NGO career however, she felt disembodied and deflated by office work. Laura experienced the need to inspire change through the heart and body, not just the mind.

After earning her Master’s in performance, Laura created [Infinite Experience](http://infiniteexperience.com/), an arts company with a mission to reveal our constructs of separation and reconnect us deeply to ourselves and each other. After journeying outward to discover the issues we face as a species, it began to be clear that much discord in fact lies within us. The resulting art piece, *Welcome Home*, has lived for over three years and travelled the world over, evolving with each person who has experienced it.

“I am beyond excited to bring my work to the Old Oak — including the aspects of my piece that have proven a successful tool in transformation and connectivity, my skills as an artist to co-create and inspire creativity and my experience in addressing trauma and conflict through ancient tools such as ritual, open-hearted listening and empathy,” said Laura.

Laura told us more about herself by finishing some sentences:

<strong><em>To me, community means…</em></strong> <em><br /></em> The future of how we will move forward into a more harmonious world.

<strong><em>It’s important to connect with people because…</em></strong> <br /> It is important to operate from the heart.

<strong><em>My biggest fear is…</em></strong><br /> Being in bed with a spider.

<strong><em>I am happiest when…</em></strong> <br /> I am in nature with those I love.

<strong><em>In the future…</em></strong><em><br /></em> I hope to do this everyday!
