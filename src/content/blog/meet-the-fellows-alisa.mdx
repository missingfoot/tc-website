Right now at The Collective Old Oak, six fellows are taking part in our first ever Community Facilitator Programme. That means they’re on the ground for the next few months, supporting our members in making their home amazing. Along the way, they’re on a peer-led journey exploring tools to foster a brilliant community experience.

We’re spotlighting each fellow in a series of profiles. Next up, it’s Alisa!

<Figure src="/images/blog/meet-the-fellows-alisa/cover.webp" alt="the collective co-living west london community" caption="“My heart can be in a moment of awe, filled with gratitude while being in the midst of these experiences. I love encountering this magic.”" />

Alisa Hentze is a poet, project developer and experience designer. Born in Ghana, half Ukrainian, she has lived in the Faroes Islands, Spain and Denmark. A multicultural child, Alisa’s background sparks her passion for working with experiences that bring people together.

She told us more about herself by finishing some sentences:

<strong>To me, community means…</strong> Being with supportive people that truly care for each others’ wellbeing.

<strong>It’s important to connect with people because…</strong> We are living in a shared experience and it just feels beautiful to feel another soul’s connection. Furthermore, each human has deep experiences that we can learn from. Through our authentic connections, we can learn, transform and grow together.

<strong>My biggest fear is…</strong> That the majority of humans lose their sense of compassion and deep connection to all of life. We become more reactive, live more in fear and allow the fear to control our lives. Creating more destruction and unconscious shit.”

<strong>I am happiest when…</strong> I’m in a regenerate culture of people.

<strong>In the future, I hope…</strong> Humanity becomes a highly regenerative species that only manifests regenerative outcomes in our world.

On why she applied to the fellowship, Alisa explained: “to be encapsulated in a moment where all of life’s magic unfolds. To explore deeply, how to hold space for the emergence of authentic deep connections. My heart can be in a moment of awe, filled with gratitude while being in the midst of these experiences. I love encountering this magic. This is an extraordinary opportunity to explore, learn and co-create such spaces for The Collective.”
