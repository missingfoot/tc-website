Right now at The Collective Old Oak, six fellows are taking part in our first ever [Community Facilitator Programme](https://medium.com/@collectivist/a-week-in-the-life-of-our-first-community-fellows-1f24119429e3). That means they’re on the ground for the next few months, supporting our members in making their community amazing. Along the way, they’re on a peer-led journey, exploring tools to foster a brilliant community experience.

We’re getting to know each fellow in our new series of profiles.

Today, meet Ashen!

<Figure src="/images/blog/meet-the-fellows-ashen/01-the-collective-old-oak-west-london-co-living-commu.webp" alt="the collective old oak west london co-living community" caption="“I hope to be able to create artwork that helps humans to evolve to their fullest potential. I have big dreams.”" />

Ashen Page is a talented and ambitious young filmmaker from London. He believes art should not just be entertainment, but therapy — meaning his films are dark and intense, aiming to provoke the audience to feel something deeper.

A strong advocate for community and connection, Ashen explained why he wanted to be a part of the Community Facilitator Programme:

“Community is fundamental for our existence and I believe it to be lost in many areas society due to the rise of the individualist. I am very excited to bring my interests of humans, philosophy, psychology and creativity to help be a part of something special, in the aim of positive and fundamental change.”

He told us more by finishing some sentences:

<strong>To me, community means…</strong> unconditional care and love for one another, no matter the differences, and creating an environment in which there is a sense of belonging.

<strong>It’s important to connect with people because…</strong> it challenges you as a human, it makes us grow. Connecting with others is just connecting to different aspects of oneself, which in many cases can be somewhat profound and beautiful.

<strong>My biggest fear…</strong> is my existence being meaningless.

<strong>I am happiest when…</strong> I am making films that challenge the status quo.

<strong>In the future…</strong> I hope to be able to create artwork that helps humans to evolve to their fullest potential. I have big dreams.
