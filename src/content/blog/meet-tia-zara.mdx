<em>We’re welcoming our first ever Community Hosts, a set of six amazing leaders helping to support our community and make it even more incredible.</em>

Today, meet Tia and Zara, two of our lovely Community Hosts at [The Collective Old Oak](/locations/old-oak)! They got to know each other by asking each other some questions and taking photos together.

### ZARA

<Figure src="/images/blog/meet-tia-zara/01-the-collective-old-oak-community-co-living.webp" alt="the collective old oak community co-living" caption="Zara’s spirit animal? A panda!" />

<strong>What does happiness mean to you?</strong>

“Feeling peaceful, being okay with yourself in your own company, feeling open and being happy to share ideas, things and anything else with others. Feeling free.”

<strong>What’s your spirit animal?</strong>

“I would say a panda bear — I like how they’re black and white, so like two extremes, but they’re also quite sensitive creature as well.”

<strong>If you were a superhero, what would be your superpower?</strong>

“Eradicating loneliness. I would see other people and my superpower would let me know what they needed.”

<strong>If you didn’t have to sleep, what would you do with the extra time?</strong>“Daydream.”

<strong>If you could turn any activity into an Olympic sport, what would you have good chances at winning a medal for?</strong>

“Being clumsy.”

### TIA

<Figure src="/images/blog/meet-tia-zara/02-the-collective-old-oak-co-living-west-london.webp" alt="the collective old oak co-living west london" caption="Tia would earn an Olympic medal in being lazy at heart, but getting shit done." />

<strong>What does happiness mean to you?</strong>

“Being okay with what you’ve got and being at peace with yourself and your surroundings. Feeling light and having freedom.”

<strong>What’s your spirit animal?</strong>

“A penguin. They have to endure a lot in tough conditions but then they are focussed and get shit done. They’re just cool! Or a dolphin because they are playful and free in the vast sea, just enjoying life and jumping around.”

<strong>If you were a superhero, what would be your superpower?</strong>

“Teleporting — being somewhere else in the blink of an eye.”

<strong>If you didn’t have to sleep, what would you do with the extra time?</strong>

“Travel and study.”

<strong>If you could turn any activity into an Olympic sport, what would you have good chances at winning a medal for?</strong>

<strong>“</strong>Being lazy at heart but getting shit done.”
