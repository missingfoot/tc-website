Pride is a time of year that we at The Collective are always excited to honour and celebrate.

Our diverse community is proud of its LGBTQ+ brothers and sisters – each of whom have their own story, and are some of the warm and colourful members of [The Collective Old Oak](/locations/old-oak).

As part of our Pride celebrations, we asked our members to take a moment to reflect on a powerful question:

### What does Pride mean to you?

![](/images/blog/the-collective-celebrates-pride/01-pride-flag-2-1024x683.webp)

Here’s what they had to say:

### <em>‘Pride is a time for acceptance, true love, and self-expression.’</em>

### -Harriet, talented linguist

![](/images/blog/the-collective-celebrates-pride/02-harriet-2-1024x683.webp)

### <em>‘The freedom to be who you are!’</em>

### – Phoebe, literary genius

### <em>‘Pride has allowed me to connect with my most authentic self.’</em> – Kevin, entrepreneur extraordinaire

![](/images/blog/the-collective-celebrates-pride/03-kevin-1024x683.webp)

> <em>‘Pride is a time to defend our freedom, to defend who we are – and to be proud of it. If we’re too afraid to be who we are out in public, we might as well be back in the closet.’</em>
>
> – Matty, fantastic photographer

![](/images/blog/the-collective-celebrates-pride/04-img-4958-2-1024x683.webp)

<em>‘Pride celebrates families all over the world.’</em>

– Devany, adventuress

![](/images/blog/the-collective-celebrates-pride/05-img-4948-copy-2-1024x683.webp)

> <em>‘To be Proud is to love.’</em>

> – Rosie, proud activist
