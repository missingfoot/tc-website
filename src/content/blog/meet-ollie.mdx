The Collective Old Oak is home to an amazing and diverse community. It brings together more than 500 people from around the world, from all walks of life. Together they create a rich network of perspectives and experiences. Continuing our new series [We Are The Collective](/blog/we-are-the-collective), our members tell us their stories.

This time, we spoke with Ollie, an apprentice at the BBC.

<Figure src="/images/blog/meet-ollie/cover.webp" alt="the collective co-living west london community" caption="Ollie, filmmaker and risk-taker." />

<strong>What makes you happy?</strong>

“Being surrounded by friends and family is what makes me most happy. Sharing the same similarities and persona is comforting and where I feel most at peace. It is difficult to be away from home but I know my family and friends will always have my back and I am supported by them on a daily basis.”

<strong>Are you happy right now?</strong>

“Yes, I am happy right now. Lately I have been meeting a lot of new people, having independence and starting the journey of pursuing my job in the creative industry. Being away from home is difficult but I am still happy and having a great time currently. I think it is important to be happy within yourself despite any circumstances that may surround you, and I constantly work to feel just that.”

<strong>What motivates you?</strong>

“What keeps me motivated is having a set goal and chasing those goals. I feel like as you are alive and in this world you should chase your goals and dreams and have something to live and fight for. I am still unsure as to what specific life I would like in the future, but I am always seeking to improve myself and my vision of success. I am sure soon I will discover what path is best suited for me.”

<Figure src="/images/blog/meet-ollie/02-the-collective-old-oak-co-living-west-london.webp" alt="the collective old oak co-living west london" caption="“I feel like as you are alive and in this world you should chase your goals and dreams and have something to live and fight for.”" />

<strong>What’s your defining trait?</strong>

“I would call myself a risk taker. I like to do things out my comfort zone and test myself which is the harder option and a risk for definite. If you don’t take risks in life, you’re not living. I think as hard as it is to take risks you must step up to the plate and take them despite the fear. I always look for the distinction between my fear and my gut and try and follow my gut.”

<strong>Why?</strong>

“Because I feel your gut is what is right for you and you only. If people aren’t happy it’s probably because their not following what is calling to them and are constantly following others’ opinions and rules and values instead of listening and recognising their own guidance system. If it’s not you making the decisions it’s not your life.”
