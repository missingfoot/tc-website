We are here to pioneer a new way of living together, and we love meeting people who are just as passionate about this as we are. So we’re excited to be welcoming our new community host team, six awesome leaders who are here to support our members at [The Collective Old Oak](/locations/old-oak).

Since joining, Rosie and Jackson have been diving into their new roles, doing lots of training, getting to know the community, and working with members to produce some great new events – like the *Live As One* talks and film screening day coming up on 19th May, in aid of Refugee Week’s *20 Simple Acts* campaign.

They got to know each other by asking each other a few random questions.

> ROSIE

<Figure src="/images/blog/meet-rosie-and-jackson/cover.webp" alt="Rosie at The Collective Old Oak" caption="Rosie at The Collective Old Oak" />

<strong>Jackson: If you won the lottery, what would you spend it on?<br /></strong> Rosie: I would invest in innovative social enterprises and donate to grassroots projects aiding the refugee crisis… whilst travelling to cool, off-the-beaten-track places!

<strong>Jackson: Name one thing you can’t live without.<br /></strong> Rosie: Podcasts! Or my dogs.

<strong>Jackson: What did you want to be when you were little?<br /></strong> Rosie: I wanted to be Kate Moss.

<strong>Jackson: What does co-living mean to you?<br /></strong> Rosie: It’s a celebration of our social nature, in an increasingly antisocial world – sad but sometimes true.

<strong>Jackson: If you were a superhero, what would be your superpower?<br /></strong> Rosie: I would love to read minds, and speak to spirits.

<strong>Jackson: What is your favourite flavour of ice cream?<br /></strong> Rosie: Ben and Jerry’s fudge brownie – non dairy!

> JACKSON

<Figure src="/images/blog/meet-rosie-and-jackson/02-jackson-at-the-collective-old-oak.webp" alt="Jackson at The Collective Old Oak" caption="Jackson at The Collective Old Oak" />

<strong>Rosie: If you won the lottery, what would you spend it on?<br /></strong> Jackson: I would retire my folks, pay off my student debt and travel! I would also try to invest it in something worthwhile that benefits society as a whole.

<strong>Rosie: Name one thing you can’t live without.<br /></strong> Jackson: Sunlight! I’m from Australia so I’m finding the London winters quite rough!

<strong>Rosie: What did you want to be when you were little?<br /></strong> Jackson: A pokemon trainer! Gotta catch ’em all.

<strong>Rosie: What does co-living mean to you?<br /></strong> Jackson: A way for people from all different walks of life to connect and share life experiences. Nobody achieves anything alone.

<strong>Rosie: If you were a superhero, what would be your superpower?<br /></strong> Jackson: Definitely flying!

<strong>Rosie: What is your favourite flavour of ice cream?<br /></strong> Jackson: Cookies and cream… can’t be beaten.
