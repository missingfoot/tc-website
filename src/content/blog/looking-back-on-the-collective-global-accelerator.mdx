Our vision at The Collective Foundation is to foster communities in cities where people thrive. We believe that investing in entrepreneurs and innovators who have the potential to positively change the world is key in achieving our vision. This is why we launched [The Collective Global Accelerator](http://www.collectiveglobalaccelerator.com/), supporting social entrepreneurs from around the world.

The programme runs over the course of a month at [The Collective Old Oak](/co-living) in London, and covers four focus areas: **Health and Wellbeing**, **Economic Opportunity**, **Social Integration** and **Environmental Sustainability**. This is because we believe that communities thrive when people live healthy lives, have the means to sustain themselves financially, are connected with those around them and live in a healthy environment.

> This year was our second time running the programme. It’s now been a few weeks since our 11 entrepreneurs left Old Oak, and we thought now would be a nice time to reflect on this year’s experience.

So what did it look like for 2018? Well, we received more than 4,500 applications from more than 160 countries, and were able to choose the best of the best. These 11 brilliant entrepreneurs lived and worked together for a month at The Collective Old Oak. We provided them with a curriculum of workshops, personal mentoring, networking and investment opportunities, and most importantly, a community to call home.

They are collectively already impacting more than 2 million people through their ventures, and we are incredibly excited to continue working with them to make an impact in communities all over the world.
