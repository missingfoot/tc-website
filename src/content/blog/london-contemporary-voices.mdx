London Contemporary Voices (LCV) is coming to The Collective! Founded in 2010 by artists Anil Sebastian and Didier Rochard, this world-leading alternative choir has since worked with more than 100 artists including Imogen Heap, Alt-J, Jarvis Cocker, and Maya Youssef. To celebrate their gig with us tonight at [The Collective Old Oak](/co-living), we asked them to collate their ultimate choir jams into a special playlist.

> Summer is a time full of festivals and fun – what better way to celebrate than with Karaoke,” LCV co-founder Anil said. “We cover a huge range of music from different eras, and here are some of our favourite guilty pleasures.

From Stevie Wonder to Queen, Prince and David Bowie, this is an icon-packed mixtape bursting with good vibes. If it’s anything like what we’re going to hear tonight, we can’t wait. Press play and hope to see you there.
