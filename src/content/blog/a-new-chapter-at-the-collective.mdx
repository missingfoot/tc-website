Few would debate that community is at the heart of co-living. It pumps blood through the system (our buildings); giving them life. Over the years, we’ve learned that the life our members desire is one of community and connection, forming a vibrant culture where they thrive together.

At The Collective, we embrace the vision of being alive together. Building affordable, high-quality homes for our members is just the beginning. To foster a nurturing community, it is the experience that creates the magic.

It all starts with values that attract a dynamic community of individuals around a shared vision of life. It can be something as simple as living passionate, fearless and balanced lives committed to growth and respect of others.

As these inspiring individuals come together, they are invited to participate in a collective experience that gives birth to communal bonds. This begins with committing to agreements that ensure a nurturing experience for all, such as respect for others, giving space when space is needed, embracing different perspectives, compassionate communication and creating safe spaces for others to share themselves free of judgement.

<strong>Now the real fun begins.</strong> Members of the community are invited to co-create experiences that promote wellness, learning and play. Imagine skill sharing sessions, dance parties, group meditation and much more. This recipe for growth transforms lives.

This is an exciting time at The Collective. We are embarking on a new chapter in our collective story; one focused on fostering a vibrant culture and nurturing community for our members. We invite you to join us in this exciting adventure.

See you soon!
