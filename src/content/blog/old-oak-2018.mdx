It’s been a big year at [The Collective Old Oak](/co-living). We’ve opened our doors for the world’s biggest architecture festival, Open House London, grown a community roof garden, celebrated Earth Day and Pride, held our own Olympics, opened a restaurant, and so much more. Then there’s everything we got up to within our events programme – from roaming bands to choirs, business talks to freelancer lunches, collage clubs to screen printing, we’ve got our hands dirty and learned a few new tricks to boot. To celebrate, we thought we’d put together a little good times flip-book with some of the highlights, in no particular order.
