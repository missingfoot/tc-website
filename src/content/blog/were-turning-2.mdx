It’s a special day for us at The Collective. Today marks two years since we welcomed our members into our first co-living building, [The Collective Old Oak](/locations/old-oak). And we’re pretty proud of how far we’ve come.

It all started with a simple idea from our CEO Reza Merchant. While studying at LSE in 2011, frustrated with the London housing market, he was inspired to change the way we live. From there The Collective was born – pioneering the concept of co-living in London. And so many years later, here we are.

> “I am immensely proud of what has been achieved at The Collective Old Oak and I can’t believe how quickly the past two years have gone,” said Reza.

“They’ve brought with them many learnings that have informed our expansion plans into the US and Germany. We are committed to improving the experience of all our members and know that as we grow, it will only get better.

I would like to say a big thank you to all our members, past and present, who have come through the doors of The Collective Old Oak. They are the ones who make the building and community what is today! I also want to thank The Collective team who have worked so hard to get us to the place we’re at today. Here’s to the next two years!”

To celebrate, we’re throwing a party tonight on the roof terrace. The team is feeling both proud of our achievement, and reflective on the work we’ve done and how much there still is to do to realise our shared vision.

Our head of community experience Ed Thomas said: “I can’t believe two years has passed since The Collective Old Oak’s doors opened. I’ll never forget the moment our first ever member Rita, moved in. She’s still living with us today!”

> In those years, we’ve learnt an immeasurable amount along the way. Today, every single member of The Collective team sees on a daily basis, the profound impact of co-living on our members’ lives. Knowing how much work it took to get us to this place makes me both very proud of our accomplishment to date but also even more excited for the future.”

We’ve got some really exciting initiatives in the pipeline here that we can’t wait to share with you soon. But for now, join us in wishing a very happy birthday to The Collective Old Oak!
