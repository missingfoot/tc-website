<em>“We realise the potential The Collective Old Oak has to become a leader in community experience, sustainability and social impact.”</em>

Right now at The Collective Old Oak, six fellows are taking part in our first ever [Community Facilitator Programme](/blog/a-week-in-the-life-of-our-first-community-fellows). That means they’re on the ground for the next few months, supporting our members in making their community amazing, while exploring tools to foster a brilliant community experience. Here they share some of their learnings so far.

<Figure src="/images/blog/ingredients-of-a-community-values/01-the-collective-old-oak-west-london-co-living-commu.webp" alt="the collective old oak west london co-living community" caption="The fellows brainstorming our community vision" />

Hello Old Oak!

The Fellowship has been going on for a few weeks now, and we are loving all the interactions, connections and experiences we are sharing with the community at Old Oak! There have been heaps of special moments with you all, listening and responding to important feedback from members about how to strengthen the community experience and personal learnings about clarity, communication, boundaries and shared living.

> <strong><em>Although a lot of our time at Old Oak has been focused on community facilitation and making the experience here as rich and engaging as possible, we have also been working on a peer-to-peer research project to distill our learnings into a manual that explores what it means to live in community.</em></strong>

Our manual will focus on several aspects of communal living, including Values, Leadership, Communications, Experience and Impact. For each of these modules we are holding workshops and presentations amongst the fellows, with the community, to The Collective Old Oak staff and at The Collective HQ. Our goal is not only to distill these learnings into a training guide but also to diffuse these practices and techniques amongst the Old Oak community and staff.

For our first module, Values, we started with a workshop on the first floor, which is currently vacant and felt like a proper space to represent a blank canvas and the start of our collaborative research work. Our Values Module Leaders, Petronella and Gabriel took us through a learning journey in which we thought of an Envisioned Future for The Collective, identified the Current Realities at Old Oak, conducted a Gap Analysis (comparing our vision and the reality) and Devised a Plan for future projects and organisational structure. This is [The Natural Step’s ABCD Process](http://www.thenaturalstep.org/our-approach/), and we applied this technique to our presentations of this Module to the Old Oak community and staff, The Collective HQ, and during our Co-living 101 workshop.

One of the key takeaways from these workshops was that although they were held with different groups of people for each session, there was a lot of alignment regarding shared values and envisioned futures for Old Oak and future spaces of The Collective. For example, one of the exercises used in each of these workshops was projecting what Old Oak would resemble in 25 years, and what kind of achievements it had made. There were a lot of shared ideas about Old Oak being a <strong>*leader in sustainability and social impact*</strong>, the creation of <strong>*groundbreaking and disruptive innovations*</strong> that occurred through connections made at Old Oak, <strong>*harmonious co-existence*</strong> between residents and staff and about Old Oak being well placed as one of the spaces in the world with the <strong>*highest rates of well-being and happiness*</strong>.

<Figure src="/images/blog/ingredients-of-a-community-values/02-the-collective-old-oak-west-london-co-living-commu.webp" alt="the collective old oak west london co-living community" caption="The Learning Lab at The Collective Old Oak" />

These workshops and presentations took us through a process of identifying and defining values within a community, which we learned occurs by collectively sensing our Core Values and Core Purpose, in order to Organise ourselves to achieve our Vision. We have started to provide the resources and learnings from our modules to the Old Oak community in the 7th floor library in our Learning Lab and during our recurring [Co-Living 101 workshop](https://www.facebook.com/events/937563159743383/). Some additional resources and theories that we will include in our manual are Simon Sinek’s [The Golden Circle](https://www.ted.com/talks/simon_sinek_how_great_leaders_inspire_action), [Presencing Institute’s Theory U](https://www.presencing.com/theoryu), [Dragon Dreaming](http://www.dragondreaming.org/), [Art of Hosting](http://www.artofhosting.org/) and [Holocracy](http://holacracy.org./). These include leadership tools and alternative organisational structures that can be used in different community settings, and are just some of the many theories and practices that we will share with the Old Oak through workshops, presentations and embodied learning exercises.

We realise the potential The Collective Old Oak has to become a leader in community experience, sustainability and social impact, and these modules are one of the ways that we are collectively moving towards these goals with the community in Old Oak!
