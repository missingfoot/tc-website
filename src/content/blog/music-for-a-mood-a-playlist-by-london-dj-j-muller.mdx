This winter, we’ve brought London’s up and coming musicians to [The Collective](/), turning our lobby into a mini-concert venue for our members. One of the standouts is DJ J Müller, who mixed it up for our CEO Reza’s 30th birthday yesterday. Her sultry sound is alternative RnB, and she always brings down the house when she performs for us.

We had her curate a playlist inspired by The Collective – have a listen and treat your ears.

> These songs have really just been the soundtrack to my life recently. Where would we be without music? They’ve all just helped me to ooze out my feelings even more. – J Müller
